Latest failure — 000000037gscnp2qanr (29 h)
-------------------------------------------
EMR `failureReason` was the generic "Job driver was killed evicted or
preempted." No OOM, no GC-overhead, no app exception, no SIGKILL in any
of our logs. Driver JVM's last activity is a shutdown hook
(SparkShutdownHookManager.runAll) cancelling the in-flight silver MERGE.
Shutdown hook firing = SIGTERM not SIGKILL = pod terminated externally.
Driver JVM then sat unresponsive for 5 h 30 m before K8s force-killed
it, despite terminationGracePeriodSeconds=600.

Two degradation curves over the 29 hours, both pointing at silver MERGE:
  - per-batch latency 30 s → 5 min (silver MERGE went from <30 s to ~4 min)
  - "task of very large size" WARN: 1.0 MiB at hour 2.5 → 5.5 MiB at hour 29


Our hypothesis
--------------
Iceberg target-table manifest metadata walked by MERGE planning is what
is being broadcast and what is growing. Silver tables have S3 Tables
maintenance configured as `icebergSnapshotManagement:
maxSnapshotAgeHours=120, minSnapshotsToKeep=1`. At 30 s commit cadence
over 29 h that is ~3,400 retained snapshots, none eligible for
expiration. We believe metadata bloat compounded driver memory pressure
until kubelet evicted the pod.

Workaround we tested that didn't work
-------------------------------------
We tried calling `CALL system.expire_snapshots(...)` from Spark against
one silver table to drive snapshot expiration ourselves (POC run
000000037h6imf7gsk3). It fails at SQL parse time with "AnalysisException:
mismatched input '(' expecting STRING" even with
IcebergSparkSessionExtensions configured. Either the extension is not
activated against the S3TablesCatalog or S3 Tables intentionally blocks
system procedures.
